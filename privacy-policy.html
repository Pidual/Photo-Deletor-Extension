Privacy Policy – Photo Deleter for Google Photos This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data whatsoever. All image processing is performed entirely locally within the user's browser using an ONNX model bundled inside the extension and executed via WebAssembly. No images, URLs, metadata, classification results, or any other information ever leave the user's device or the extension itself. There are no external servers, no backend, no analytics, and no third-party services involved. The extension only reads images already loaded in the browser's memory to classify them locally and simulate user interactions with the Google Photos interface. Contact: carlosmapache1@gmail.com
